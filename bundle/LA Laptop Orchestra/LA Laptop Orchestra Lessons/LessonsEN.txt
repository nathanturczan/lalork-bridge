Use this Live Set for both rehearsal and performance. The room you join determines which harmony the instruments follow.

*Rehearsal*

Open $Link rehearse.laptoporchestra.com <https://rehearse.laptoporchestra.com>. A private rehearsal room is created just for you. As the 12-minute piece advances, its scale, chord, and tempo change at the scored timecodes.

Copy your rehearsal code, paste it into the *Rehearsal* tab, and click *Join*.

*Live Performance*

Open $Link enter.laptoporchestra.com <https://enter.laptoporchestra.com>. Switch to the *Performance* tab—it auto-connects to the live ensemble.

All devices in this Set share one connection. Each track has its own Output setting.
